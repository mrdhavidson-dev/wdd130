Dave's Rafting Company

At Dave's Rafting Company our mission is to provide safe, unforgettable, and thrilling river adventures that connect people with the power of nature. We are dedicated to delivering exceptional outdoor experience through expert guides, top-tier equipment, and an unwavering commitment to safety and environmental stewardship.Motto: Ride the Waves, Make Memories!

History

Founded with a deep passion for the outdoors and white-water exploration. Daves's Rafting Company started out a small team of adventurous river guides looking to share the thrill of the rapids with others. Over the years, we have grown from a modest local operation into a premier destination for river enthusiasts, while always maintaining our core commitment to safety, teamwork, and unforgetable customer experiences on the water.
