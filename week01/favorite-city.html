My Favorite City

Accra is my favorite city.It has so many unique and natural features that make it stand out from other cities. There are numerous places you can visit in Accra if you are considering a vacation. Here is a list of some places you can visit while you are in Accra. You can visit the zoo to see wild animals like lions, zebras, monkeys, and many other animals you probably have not seen in your life. You can also visit the National Museum to see historical arts, objects and learn about some historical facts about the city.The National Theatre, Kwame Nkrumah Memorial Park, Independence Square, Labadi Beach , Osu and many other places.
